Family pension rules today (7th CPC)

Family pension for central civil employees is governed by Rule 50 of the CCS (Pension) Rules, 2021. There are two rates, both calculated on the last pay drawn (basic pay) of the employee:

RateAmountLimitsWhen it applies
Normal (ordinary)30% of last payMin ₹9,000 · Max ₹75,000Default rate; also applies after the enhanced period ends
Enhanced50% of last payMin ₹9,000 · Max ₹1,25,000For a limited period after death (see below)

How long the enhanced rate lasts

  • Death while in service: enhanced rate for 10 years from the day after death. Since 1 October 2019 there is no minimum service requirement — the earlier condition of seven years' continuous service was removed.
  • Death after retirement: enhanced rate for seven years, or until the date the deceased would have turned 67, whichever is earlier. It cannot exceed the pension that was authorised to the pensioner. DoPPW clarified in October 2025 that this applies whatever the retirement age.

After the enhanced period, family pension falls to the normal 30% rate. Your Pension Payment Order (PPO) shows both amounts and the date the enhanced rate ends.

Additional family pension for older family pensioners

Family pensioners aged 80 and above get additional family pension on the basic family pension: 20% from 80, 30% from 85, 40% from 90, 50% from 95 and 100% from 100 years, payable from the first day of the month in which the age is reached. Dearness Relief (60% since 1 January 2026) is paid on basic family pension plus additional family pension. Enter the family pensioner's age in the calculator and it is added automatically.

How the 8th CPC revision is estimated

Under the 7th CPC, family pension was revised the same way as pension: the family pension being drawn was multiplied by 2.57 and DR restarted at zero. This calculator applies your chosen fitment factor to your current basic family pension, raises it to an estimated new minimum (₹9,000 × factor) if needed, and limits it to the estimated new maximum for that rate. For example, a widow drawing the minimum ₹9,000 family pension at age 70 receives ₹14,400 today with DR. At a factor of 2.57 the estimated new family pension is ₹23,130; at 2.86 it is ₹25,740.

If you are currently drawing enhanced family pension and it is due to drop to the normal rate before the revision, run the calculator twice — once with the enhanced amount and once with the normal amount — to see both phases.

Working out family pension from last pay

If you do not have the PPO at hand, the helper box above calculates both rates from the employee's last basic pay under the 7th CPC (for example ₹56,100 gives ₹16,830 normal and ₹28,050 enhanced). Use the button to copy the figure into the calculator. If the employee retired or died before 2016, use the revised 7th CPC family pension shown on your latest PPO or bank statement instead, because pre-2016 amounts were already revised.

Common questions from family pensioners

Many family pensioners worry whether the 8th CPC covers them. The Commission's Terms of Reference include retirement benefits, and the Government has stated that pensions are within its scope; family pensions have been revised along with pensions after every past pay commission. A viral claim that pensioners would stop getting pay commission revisions under the Finance Act 2025 was declared fake by PIB Fact Check on 13 November 2025. As with all figures here, wait for the official order before relying on any amount, and never share OTPs or bank details with anyone offering to "calculate" your pension.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum family pension under the 7th CPC?

₹9,000 per month, plus Dearness Relief (60% from 1 January 2026). The maximum is ₹75,000 at the normal rate and ₹1,25,000 at the enhanced rate.

What is the difference between normal and enhanced family pension?

Normal family pension is 30% of the employee's last basic pay. Enhanced family pension is 50% of last pay and is paid for a limited period: 10 years after a death in service, or seven years / until the deceased would have turned 67 (whichever is earlier) after a death following retirement.

Will family pensioners get the 8th Pay Commission benefit?

Family pensions have been revised along with pensions after every past pay commission, and pension matters are within the 8th CPC's Terms of Reference. The method and amounts will be known only after the report and Government orders.

Is additional pension given to family pensioners above 80?

Yes. Additional family pension is 20% at 80, 30% at 85, 40% at 90, 50% at 95 and 100% at 100 years, on the basic family pension, with DR on top.

How much will ₹9,000 family pension become in the 8th CPC?

At 2.57 it would be about ₹23,130; at 2.86, ₹25,740; at 3.0, ₹27,000. These are estimates only; no factor has been announced.
