All results are estimates

As of 28 October 2026, the 8th Central Pay Commission has not submitted its report, and the Government has not announced a fitment factor, a revised pension structure, a new minimum or maximum pension, or a date of effect. Every figure produced by the calculators, charts and examples on this website is therefore a hypothetical estimate based on:

  • the fitment factor you select, none of which is official;
  • the method used by the 7th Pay Commission in 2016, which the 8th CPC may or may not follow;
  • an assumed new minimum and maximum pension (current limit × fitment factor);
  • an assumption that Dearness Relief restarts at 0% on the revised pension;
  • an assumed date of effect (1 January 2026 by default) and a payment month that you choose;
  • the current DR rate entered, applied for the whole arrears period.

Your actual revised pension, family pension and arrears will be decided only by official Government orders and fixed by your Pension Disbursing Authority. They may differ substantially from any estimate here.

Defence, railway and other pensioners

The calculation follows the general method for central civil pensioners. Armed forces, railway, autonomous body, state government and bank pensioners may be governed by different rules, orders or timelines. In particular, disability and war injury elements, OROP revisions and state government pay commissions are not covered.

Frequently asked questions

Is the 8th Pay Commission fitment factor on this site official?

No. None of the fitment factors is official as of 28 October 2026. They are scenarios for estimation.

Can I rely on the arrears estimate?

Only as a rough guide. Actual arrears depend on the official date of effect, the revised pension and the DR actually drawn each month.

Who decides my actual revised pension?

Your Pension Disbursing Authority, following orders issued by the Government after it accepts the Pay Commission's recommendations.
