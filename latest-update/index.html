Timeline

  1. January 2025The Government announces that the 8th Central Pay Commission will be set up. Official
  2. 28 October 2025Union Cabinet approves the Terms of Reference. The Commission is a temporary body with a Chairperson, one part-time Member and a Member-Secretary, and must report within 18 months. It must keep in view fiscal prudence, resources for development and welfare, the unfunded cost of non-contributory pension schemes, the impact on state finances, and pay in CPSUs and the private sector. PIB adds that the effect "would normally be expected from 01.01.2026". Official
  3. 3 November 2025Commission constituted by Government Resolution, chaired by Justice Ranjana Prakash Desai. Official
  4. 13 November 2025PIB Fact Check marks as fake a viral claim that, under the Finance Act 2025, retired employees would no longer get DA hikes or pay commission revisions. Official
  5. December 2025Parliament is told there is no proposal under consideration to merge DA with basic pay. Official
  6. 18 April 2026Cabinet approves a 2% increase in DA and DR, from 58% to 60% with effect from 1 January 2026, benefiting about 68.27 lakh pensioners. DoPPW issues the DR order on 24 April 2026 (OM No. 42/02/2024-P&PW(D)/E-9475); DR is paid on basic pension including additional pension. Official
  7. May 2026Consultations with employee bodies under way. The Indian Railway Technical Supervisors' Association proposes graded fitment factors from 2.92 (Levels 1–5) to 4.38 (Levels 17–18). Demand
  8. 15 June 2026Last date for memorandums to the Commission (extended). Official
  9. July–August 2026Replying in Parliament, the Government says the Commission has not yet submitted recommendations, so the fitment factor and implementation date cannot be stated. Official (as reported)
  10. August–September 2026Commission visits Jaipur (31 August–1 September) and Chandigarh (16–18 September). NC-JCM staff side's demand of ₹69,000 minimum pay and a 3.833 factor is widely reported. Official visits / Demand
  11. 30 September 2026Labour Bureau releases CPI-IW for August 2026 at 154.4. Official
  12. 7–8 October 2026Commission's consultations in Bengaluru. A pensioners' association seeks a 3.833 factor and higher annual increments. Official visit / Demand
  13. 22–23 October 2026Commission's consultations in Mumbai. No further visits had been listed for November–December when checked on 23 October. Official
  14. 28 October 2026July 2026 DA/DR instalment still not announced (pib.gov.in release list checked at about 2:00 pm IST); DR remains 60%. No fitment factor, pension matrix or date of effect. Use the DA/DR calculator to see what a 3-point rise would mean. Official position

What pensioners should expect next

The Commission may send interim reports if it chooses, but its final report is due around May 2027. Past experience suggests that implementation follows a few months after the report: the 7th CPC reported in November 2015, the Cabinet approved it in June 2016, and revised pensions with arrears from 1 January 2016 were paid later that year. Meanwhile, Dearness Relief continues to be revised twice a year under the 7th CPC formula, so the July 2026 and January 2027 instalments still matter for your current pension and for any arrears calculation.

How to verify an announcement

  • A genuine decision first appears as a Cabinet release on pib.gov.in.
  • For pensioners, the Department of Pension & Pensioners' Welfare (doppw.gov.in) then issues an office memorandum; for armed forces pensioners, the Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare.
  • The Commission's own notices are on 8cpc.gov.in.
  • Screenshots, YouTube thumbnails and forwarded PDFs without a link to these sites are not orders.

Frequently asked questions

Has the 8th Pay Commission submitted its report?

No. As of 28 October 2026 the report has not been submitted. It is due within 18 months of 3 November 2025, around May 2027.

What is the current DR for central government pensioners?

60% of basic pension (including additional pension) from 1 January 2026, approved by the Cabinet on 18 April 2026.

Has the July 2026 DA/DR been announced?

Not as of 28 October 2026. The CPI-IW formula suggests about 63%, but that is a calculation, not an announcement.

Who heads the 8th Pay Commission?

Justice Ranjana Prakash Desai is the Chairperson. Prof. Pulak Ghosh is the part-time Member and Shri Pankaj Jain is the Member-Secretary.
