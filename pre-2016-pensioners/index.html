Pre-2016 Pensioners: Both 7th CPC Methods and the 8th CPC Estimate

If you retired, or your family pension started, before 1 January 2016, your current pension was fixed by the higher of two methods. Check which one applies to you, then see what the 8th Pay Commission could mean.

Estimate — not official. The 8th Pay Commission has not announced a fitment factor.

Pre-2016 pensioner calculator

The two methods used by the 7th CPC

The 7th Central Pay Commission recommended two "formulations" for revising the pension of people who retired before 2016. The Government implemented them in two steps, and every pre-2016 central civil pensioner is entitled to whichever gives the higher pension.

Method A — the 2.57 multiplier (DoPPW OM dated 4 August 2016)

The pension or family pension "as had been fixed at the time of implementation of 6th CPC recommendations" was multiplied by 2.57 and rounded off to the next higher rupee, with effect from 1 January 2016. The existing pension here is the basic pension including any commuted portion and excluding additional pension for age. Anything below ₹9,000 was stepped up to ₹9,000. Banks applied this automatically, without a fresh authority.

Method B — notional pay fixation (DoPPW OM dated 12 May 2017)

The pensioner's pay is fixed notionally in the 7th CPC pay matrix, at the level corresponding to the pay scale (or pay band and grade pay) at which they retired, by carrying it through each intervening pay commission's fixation formula. 50% of that notional pay as on 01.01.2016 is the revised pension; 30% is the normal family pension and 50% the enhanced family pension, each rounded up to the next rupee. The OM then says that "higher of the two Formulations" shall be granted. Unlike Method A, this revision is done by the Head of Department and Pay & Accounts Office, who issue a revised authority under the existing PPO number.

Same limits for both: minimum ₹9,000 (excluding additional pension); maximum 50% of the highest pay of ₹2,50,000 for pension (₹1,25,000) and 30% for family pension (₹75,000). Additional pension for age (20% at 80 … 100% at 100) is added on top of the revised basic.

Why the calculator asks for your notional pay instead of working it out

Method B needs your exact pay scale on retirement, the fixation rules of every pay commission since then, and the concordance tables that DoPPW circulated on 6 July 2017 to map old scales to 7th CPC levels. Getting any one of those wrong gives a confident but wrong answer, so this site does not guess it. Instead:

  • Enter your notional pay as on 01.01.2016 if it is written on your revised PPO or revision authority. The calculator then compares both methods.
  • If you don't have it, leave it blank: you will see Method A, which is the minimum you are entitled to.
  • The Department of Pension's Pensioners' Portal has an official "Revised Pension Calculator for pre-2016 Pensioners" that uses the concordance tables; you can use it to find your notional figure.

What the 8th Pay Commission could mean for pre-2016 pensioners

Whatever method fixed your pension, the result became your basic pension from 1 January 2016, and Dearness Relief has been paid on it since (60% from 1 January 2026). The calculator's 8th CPC figure applies the fitment factor you choose to that basic pension — the same approach the 7th CPC took for everyone through Method A. It is an estimate: no fitment factor has been announced as of 28 October 2026, and nobody yet knows whether the 8th CPC will again offer older pensioners a notional-pay option, or a different method altogether. Pensioner associations have raised the treatment of older pensioners in their submissions; the Commission's report is due around May 2027.

For defence pensioners, note that the two OMs above apply to central civil pensioners (including CAPFs, railway and All India Services pensioners). Armed forces pensions were revised through separate Ministry of Defence orders; see the defence pension page.

Who this page is for

Central civil pensioners and family pensioners whose pension started before 1 January 2016, including CAPF, railway and All India Services pensioners. It is also useful if you suspect your pension was never revised under the 12 May 2017 notional-pay order.

How to use the calculator

  1. Enter your pension as on 31.12.2015 (before the 2.57 revision), from your old PPO or the bank's revision entry.
  2. If your revised authority shows notional pay as on 01.01.2016, enter it to compare Method B.
  3. Enter age and pension type, choose an 8th CPC factor and calculate.
  4. If Method B is higher but you are still being paid Method A, write to your Head of Department / Pay and Accounts Office with a copy of the OM dated 12.05.2017, or file a grievance on CPENGRAMS (pgportal.gov.in).

Common mistakes

  • Multiplying today's basic by 2.57 again. The 2.57 revision already happened in 2016; today's basic is the result.
  • Including additional pension before multiplying. Only basic pension was multiplied.
  • Expecting arrears before 2016. The 2017 OM allowed none before 01.01.2016.

Frequently asked questions

How was pension revised for pre-2016 pensioners in the 7th CPC?

By the higher of two methods: the pension fixed under the 6th CPC multiplied by 2.57 (DoPPW OM 04.08.2016), or 50% of notional pay fixed in the 7th CPC pay matrix as on 01.01.2016 (DoPPW OM 12.05.2017). Family pension used 30% (normal) or 50% (enhanced) of notional pay.

Does the 2.57 multiplier include the commuted portion?

Yes. The existing pension for this purpose is the basic pension inclusive of the commuted portion. The commuted amount continues to be deducted from monthly disbursements until restoration.

Is additional pension for age included before multiplying by 2.57?

No. Only basic pension is multiplied. Additional pension for those aged 80 and above is then calculated on the revised basic pension.

How do I know my notional pay as on 01.01.2016?

It appears in the revised pension authority issued by your Head of Department / Pay and Accounts Office under your existing PPO number. The Pensioners' Portal also has an official revised-pension calculator for pre-2016 pensioners.

Will the 8th Pay Commission use the same two methods?

Not known. The 8th CPC has not reported yet. This calculator shows an estimate based on your current basic pension multiplied by the fitment factor you choose.

Were arrears paid for notional fixation before 2016?

No. The 12.05.2017 OM says no arrears are admissible for any period before 01.01.2016; arrears from 01.01.2016 were paid after adjusting what had already been paid under the 2.57 method.

My pension was never revised by notional pay fixation. What can I do?

Ask your Head of Department / Pay and Accounts Office for the revised authority under the OM dated 12.05.2017. If there is no reply, file a pension grievance on CPENGRAMS through pgportal.gov.in.

Do the 2016 and 2017 orders apply to armed forces pensioners?

No. Both OMs say separate orders will be issued by the Ministry of Defence for armed forces pensioners and family pensioners.

Did banks revise pre-2016 pensions automatically?

Banks applied the 2.57 multiplier automatically under the 04.08.2016 OM. The notional-pay revision needed a revised authority from the Head of Department / PAO under the existing PPO number.

Is the minimum ₹9,000 applied before or after additional pension?

The minimum ₹9,000 excludes additional pension. Additional pension for age is then added on the revised basic.
