₹80,000 Pension in 8th Pay Commission – How Much Will It Be?

A ₹80,000 basic pension is estimated to become ₹1,53,600 to ₹3,06,640 a month under the 8th Pay Commission (fitment factor 1.92 to 3.833). At 2.57, the factor the 7th CPC used, it would be ₹2,05,600. Today the same pension pays ₹1,30,400 with 63% DR, so the real monthly gain at 2.57 is about ₹75,200. No factor is official yet.

Estimate — not official. The 8th Pay Commission has not announced a fitment factor.

The range is wide because the factor is unknown. At the low-end media estimate of 1.92, the gain over today's ₹1,30,400 is only ₹23,200 a month; at 2.86 it is ₹98,400; at the staff-side demand of 3.833 it is ₹1,76,240. Any factor below 1.63 would leave a ₹80,000 pensioner with less than today, because the 63% DR is expected to be merged into the new basic.

Worked example: ₹80,000 at a factor of 2.57

  1. Pension today: ₹80,000 basic + 63% DR (₹50,400) = ₹1,30,400 a month.
  2. New basic pension: ₹80,000 × 2.57 = ₹2,05,600 (rounded up to the next rupee).
  3. DR after revision: assumed to restart at 0%, as after the 6th and 7th CPC, so the new total is ₹2,05,600.
  4. Monthly gain: ₹2,05,600 − ₹1,30,400 = ₹75,200 (about 58%).
  5. Later DR instalments would be added on ₹2,05,600 from the first DR order after revision.

Aged 80 or more? Additional pension of 20% is added before DR. Today that makes ₹80,000 + ₹16,000 + DR ₹60,480 = ₹1,56,480. At 2.57 it would be ₹2,05,600 + ₹41,120 = ₹2,46,720. Higher slabs apply at 85, 90, 95 and 100.

Estimated arrears on ₹80,000 pension

The site assumes the revision takes effect from January 2026 (the Government said that is "normally expected" from past practice; it is not decided) and that the new pension is first paid in October 2027. That gives 21 months of arrears, January 2026 to September 2027. On ₹80,000, arrears would be about ₹15,79,200 at 2.57, ₹4,87,200 at 1.92 and ₹37,01,040 at 3.833, before tax. If DR rises again before implementation, today's total goes up and arrears come down. Change both dates in the main calculator.

If you commuted 40% of ₹80,000

Commuting 40% means a fixed deduction of ₹32,000 a month until restoration after 15 years. DR is still paid on the full ₹80,000, so today the credit is ₹1,30,400 − ₹32,000 = ₹98,400 (before Fixed Medical Allowance and tax). In the 7th CPC revision the full pension was multiplied and the deduction stayed at the same rupee amount (DoPPW OM 04.08.2016). If that repeats, the credit at 2.57 would be ₹2,05,600 − ₹32,000 = ₹1,73,600, and the full ₹2,05,600 once the commuted part is restored. Details: commutation calculator.

Family pension after a ₹80,000 pension

Normal family pension is 30% of the employee's last pay, not of the pension. If ₹80,000 was 50% of a last pay of ₹1,60,000, the family pension would be ₹48,000 a month today, or ₹78,240 with 63% DR. At 2.57 that becomes ₹1,23,360 (range ₹92,160 to ₹1,83,984). For a limited period the enhanced rate of 50% of last pay — the full ₹80,000 — may apply instead. Use the family pension calculator with the exact last pay from the PPO.

Who gets a ₹80,000 pension?

A pensioner who retired after 1 January 2016 with at least 10 years' qualifying service gets 50% of last pay (or of the average of the last 10 months' pay, if that is higher). So ₹80,000 is typically the pension of someone whose last basic pay was about ₹1,60,000. For someone who retired before 2016, ₹80,000 usually means a 6th CPC pension of about ₹31,128 that was multiplied by 2.57 in 2016. If you are not sure, the basic pension finder works it out from your bank credit or PPO.

Frequently asked questions

How much will ₹80,000 pension become in the 8th Pay Commission?

An estimated ₹1,53,600 to ₹3,06,640 a month as basic pension, at fitment factors 1.92 to 3.833. At 2.57 it would be ₹2,05,600. No factor has been announced.

How much will my ₹80,000 pension actually increase each month?

Today ₹80,000 pays ₹1,30,400 with 63% DR. If DR restarts at zero, the gain is ₹23,200 at 1.92, ₹75,200 at 2.57 and ₹1,76,240 at 3.833 a month.

How much arrears will I get on ₹80,000 pension?

About ₹15,79,200 at 2.57 if the revision is effective from January 2026 and first paid in October 2027 (21 months). Both dates are assumptions, not decisions.

I commuted 40% of my ₹80,000 pension. What will I get after the 8th CPC?

The ₹32,000 deduction is expected to stay the same until restoration. At 2.57 the credit would be about ₹1,73,600 against ₹98,400 today, before FMA and tax.

What family pension will my spouse get on a ₹80,000 pension?

Normal family pension is 30% of last pay. If ₹80,000 was 50% of a last pay of ₹1,60,000, it is ₹48,000 today and about ₹1,23,360 at 2.57.

I am over 80 with ₹80,000 basic pension. What will I get?

With 20% additional pension at 80–84, today's total is ₹1,56,480. At 2.57 it would be ₹2,46,720 before any new DR.

Will DR be added on top of the new ₹80,000 pension?

Not at the start. Past pay commissions merged DR into the new basic and restarted it at 0%. New DR instalments would then be paid on the revised basic, e.g. ₹2,05,600 at 2.57.
