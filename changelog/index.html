How we log changes

An entry is added whenever a rate changes (for example a new DR instalment), an official order is checked, a calculation is corrected or a page is added. Each rate change names the order it comes from, and the "data last updated" date at the top of every page changes with it. Formulas and sources are on How we calculate; our verification and corrections rules are in the editorial policy; announcements by the Government and the Commission are tracked on the latest update page.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know the calculator uses the latest DR?

The current DR and the date it was last checked are shown in the status strip on every page and logged here with the order number.

Will the site be updated when the fitment factor is announced?

Yes. The official factor becomes the default in every calculator and the change is logged here the same day.
