Frequently asked questions

Which amount should I pick?

Your basic pension as written on the PPO or pension slip — before DR, additional pension and any commutation deduction.

Are these figures official?

No. The 8th Pay Commission has not recommended a fitment factor. Every figure is an estimate using the 7th CPC method.

Why is the gain smaller than the jump in basic pension?

Because today's 63% DR is expected to be merged into the new basic and restart at zero. A factor of 1.63 would only keep the total the same.

Do the pages work for family pension?

The table works for any basic amount, including a basic family pension; each page also explains the family pension that follows a retirement pension.
