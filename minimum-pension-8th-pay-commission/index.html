Minimum Pension under the 8th Pay Commission

Central government pensioners and family pensioners on the minimum get ₹9,000 basic plus DR today. See how the minimum has moved in past pay commissions and what each 8th CPC fitment factor would make it.

The minimum pension today

Since 1 January 2016 the minimum pension and minimum family pension for central government civil pensioners is ₹9,000 a month, excluding the additional pension paid to pensioners aged 80 and above (DoPPW OM dated 04.08.2016, para 4.4). The 7th CPC orders raised it from ₹3,500 — PIB listed the increase "from Rs.3500/- per month to Rs.9000/- per month" among DoPPW's reforms. With Dearness Relief at 60% from 1 January 2026, a pensioner on the minimum receives ₹14,400 a month (₹9,000 + ₹5,400 DR), plus additional pension if 80 or older and Fixed Medical Allowance of ₹1,000 if living outside a CGHS area.

The maximum, for comparison, is 50% of the highest pay in the Government — ₹1,25,000 — and ₹75,000 for normal family pension.

What the minimum could become (estimates)

In 2016 the minimum went up by exactly the fitment factor: ₹3,500 × 2.57 = ₹8,995, rounded to ₹9,000. If the 8th CPC again applies one factor across the board, the new minimum would be ₹9,000 × factor. None of these factors is official.

The 7th CPC also set the minimum by first fixing minimum pay (₹18,000) and taking 50% of it. Staff-side bodies have proposed a minimum pay of ₹69,000 for the 8th CPC; 50% of that would be ₹34,500, close to ₹9,000 × 3.833 = ₹34,497. That is a demand, not a decision.

Who is on the minimum pension?

  • Many family pensioners — 30% of a low last pay is often below ₹9,000, so the minimum applies.
  • Pensioners who retired from lower pay levels long ago, whose 2.57 revision fell below ₹9,000 and was stepped up.
  • Some armed forces pensioners and family pensioners of lower ranks (the ₹9,000 minimum applied to them under the 7th CPC too).

Common mistakes

  • Comparing the new minimum with ₹9,000. Compare it with ₹14,400 — what you receive today with DR.
  • Including additional pension in the minimum. The minimum excludes it; additional pension is paid on top.
  • Assuming the minimum is announced. The 8th CPC has not recommended a minimum as of 28 October 2026.

Minimum pensioners aged 80 and above

Additional pension is paid on top of the ₹9,000 minimum, so very old pensioners on the minimum receive more than ₹14,400 today, and the additional pension would rise with any new minimum. For a 100-year-old pensioner on the minimum, additional pension doubles the basic: ₹9,000 + ₹9,000 + 60% DR = ₹28,800 today. At 2.57 the estimate is ₹46,260 before any new DR.

How to check whether you are on the minimum

  • Look at the basic pension on your PPO or pension slip. If it is exactly ₹9,000, you are on the minimum.
  • If you only know the bank credit, use the basic pension finder: ₹14,400 for a pensioner below 80 with no deductions points to ₹9,000 basic.
  • Family pensioners: if 30% of the employee's last pay was less than ₹9,000, the minimum applies.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum pension for central government pensioners now?

₹9,000 a month from 1 January 2016, excluding additional pension for age, plus DR (60% from 1 January 2026) — a total of ₹14,400 for a pensioner below 80.

What will the minimum pension be under the 8th Pay Commission?

Not announced. If a single factor is applied as in 2016, it would be ₹9,000 × factor: ₹17,280 at 1.92, ₹23,130 at 2.57, ₹25,740 at 2.86 or ₹34,497 at 3.833.

Is the minimum family pension also ₹9,000?

Yes. Minimum family pension is ₹9,000 a month, plus DR.

What was the minimum pension under the 6th CPC?

₹3,500 a month. The 7th CPC orders raised it to ₹9,000 from 1 January 2016.

Does additional pension at 80+ count towards the minimum?

No. The ₹9,000 minimum excludes additional pension, which is paid on top of the basic.

Do armed forces pensioners get the same minimum?

Under the 7th CPC the ₹9,000 minimum pension applied to armed forces pensioners as well, through Ministry of Defence orders.

Is the ₹69,000 minimum pay demand official?

No. It is the reported demand of the NC-JCM staff side, which gives the 3.833 factor. It has no official status.

Do minimum pensioners get Fixed Medical Allowance?

If they live in a non-CGHS area and do not use CGHS for OPD, yes — ₹1,000 a month, without DR.
