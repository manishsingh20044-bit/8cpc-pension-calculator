About this calculator

An independent, free tool that helps central government pensioners and family pensioners understand what the 8th Pay Commission could mean for them.

Estimate — not official. The 8th Pay Commission has not announced a fitment factor.

Why this site exists

Every pay commission brings a flood of headlines, WhatsApp forwards and YouTube thumbnails promising a "confirmed" new pension. Most of them mix up demands, estimates and actual orders, and very few explain the arithmetic. Pensioners — many of them in their seventies, eighties and nineties — deserve a clear, honest way to see what different outcomes would mean for their own monthly pension, without signing up, downloading an app or sharing personal details.

This site was built by Manish, an independent developer in India, to do exactly that: a fast calculator that runs entirely in your browser, written in plain English and Hindi, with every assumption shown on screen.

What we do

  • Calculate transparently. Each result shows the step-by-step formula, so you can check it with a pen and paper.
  • Label everything. Every result is marked "Estimate — not official". Figures on the latest update page are labelled Official, Demand or Estimate.
  • Use primary sources. Current rules — the ₹9,000 minimum pension, the ₹1,25,000 maximum, family pension rates, additional pension for age and the 60% Dearness Relief — are taken from the CCS (Pension) Rules, 2021, DoPPW office memoranda and Cabinet releases on pib.gov.in.
  • Update quickly. All official numbers sit in a single settings block in the site's code, so when the Government announces the fitment factor or a new DR rate, the calculator can be updated within minutes. The date of the last update is shown at the top of every page.

What we don't do

  • We are not a government website and are not connected with the Government of India, DoPPW, CPAO, the Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare, any bank or the 8th Central Pay Commission.
  • We do not ask for your name, PPO number, bank account, Aadhaar, PAN or OTP — ever. Anyone who asks for these in our name is a fraud.
  • We do not store the numbers you type. The calculation happens on your own device.
  • We do not predict the final fitment factor. We let you test scenarios.

How the calculation works

The method follows the way the 7th Pay Commission revised pensions in 2016: your basic pension is multiplied by a fitment factor and rounded up to the next rupee; additional pension for age is applied at the same percentage; and Dearness Relief is assumed to restart at zero on the new pension. Arrears are estimated as the monthly increase multiplied by the number of months between the assumed date of effect and the month the revised pension is first paid. The full list of assumptions is shown under every result and explained on the calculator page.

This approach is a reasonable first estimate, but the 8th CPC may recommend something different — for example a separate method for older pensioners, a new minimum pension or changes to the age slabs. When that happens, the calculator will be changed to match the official orders.

Corrections

If you spot a mistake — a wrong rule, a broken calculation or an outdated date — please write to us through the contact page with a link to the official order if you have one. Verified corrections are made promptly and the "last updated" date is changed.

Accessibility and languages

The site is designed to work on low-cost phones and slow connections: no external fonts, no tracking libraries and no heavy images. Text can be enlarged with your browser's zoom, form fields have clear labels, and the whole interface can be switched to Hindi with one tap. A full Hindi guide is available at 8वां वेतन आयोग पेंशन कैलकुलेटर.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a government website?

No. It is an independent website run by a private individual and is not affiliated with any government body.

Is the calculator free?

Yes. It is free to use and needs no registration.

How often is the data updated?

Whenever a relevant official order or announcement appears, such as a DR instalment or 8th CPC decision. The last update date is shown on every page.
