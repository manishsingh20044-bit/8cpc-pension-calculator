About this calculator

An independent, free tool that helps central government pensioners and family pensioners understand what the 8th Pay Commission could mean for them.

Estimate — not official. The 8th Pay Commission has not announced a fitment factor.

Last verified: 30 Oct 2026 against official orders · Sources

Who runs this site

This website is run by the 8CPC Pension Calculator team, a small independent team in India that builds free, privacy-friendly tools. We are not connected with any government body, union, bank or political party, and we do not sell financial products. We publish no personal names or phone numbers for the team on purpose: questions and corrections are handled through the contact page, and the work speaks through its sources and its corrections record.

Why this site exists

Every pay commission brings a flood of headlines, WhatsApp forwards and YouTube thumbnails promising a "confirmed" new pension. Most of them mix up demands, estimates and actual orders, and very few explain the arithmetic. Pensioners — many of them in their seventies, eighties and nineties — deserve a clear, honest way to see what different outcomes would mean for their own monthly pension, without signing up, downloading an app or sharing personal details.

So we built fast calculators that run entirely in your browser, written in plain English and simple Hindi, with every assumption shown on screen. Every page exists in both languages.

What we do

  • Calculate transparently. Each result shows the step-by-step formula, so you can check it with a pen and paper.
  • Label everything. Every 8th CPC result is marked "Estimate — not official". Figures on the latest update page are labelled Official, Demand or Estimate.
  • Use primary sources. Current rules come from the CCS (Pension) Rules, DoPPW office memoranda and Cabinet releases on pib.gov.in, linked in a Sources box on every tool page with the date we last checked them.
  • Correct quickly and openly. Our editorial policy explains how we verify and correct; every change is dated on the changelog.
  • Update on announcement day. All official numbers sit in one settings block, so a new DR or an official fitment factor can be reflected across the site within minutes.

What we don't do

  • We are not a government website and are not connected with the Government of India, DoPPW, CPAO, the Department of Ex-Servicemen Welfare, any bank or the 8th Central Pay Commission. We do not use the State Emblem or any ministry logo.
  • We never ask for your name, PPO number, bank account, Aadhaar, PAN or OTP. Anyone who asks for these in our name is a fraud.
  • We do not store the numbers you type. The calculation happens on your own device.
  • We do not predict the final fitment factor. We let you test scenarios.
  • We do not accept payment to change any figure, ranking or wording.

How the calculation works

The method follows the way the 7th Pay Commission revised pensions in 2016: your basic pension is multiplied by a fitment factor and rounded up to the next rupee; additional pension for age is applied at the same percentage; and Dearness Relief is assumed to restart at zero on the new pension. Arrears are estimated as the monthly increase multiplied by the number of months between the assumed date of effect and the month the revised pension is first paid. Full details are on How we calculate.

Accessibility and languages

The site is designed to work on low-cost phones and slow connections: no external fonts, only a lightweight analytics tag and no heavy images. Text can be enlarged with your browser's zoom, form fields have clear labels, and every page has a full Hindi version — tap "हिंदी" at the top of any page.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a government website?

No. It is an independent website run by the 8CPC Pension Calculator team and is not affiliated with any government body.

Who writes and checks the content?

The 8CPC Pension Calculator team. Every rule is checked against the official order linked on the page, and the date of the last check is shown at the top of each tool page.

Is the calculator free?

Yes. It is free to use and needs no registration.

How often is the data updated?

Whenever a relevant official order or announcement appears, such as a DR instalment or an 8th CPC decision. The last update date is shown on every page.

Why don't you publish a name or phone number?

To keep the focus on verifiable sources and to avoid fraudsters impersonating a person. All queries go through the contact page by email.
