The DoPPW order of 24 April 2026 raised DR "from the existing rate of 58% to 60% of the basic pension/family pension (including additional pension/additional family pension)" with effect from 1 January 2026, for all central government pensioners and family pensioners, including armed forces, railway and All India Services pensioners. It also says that a fraction of a rupee in DR is rounded up to the next rupee.

On 28 October 2026 the Union Cabinet approved an additional instalment of DR to pensioners with effect from 1 July 2026, "representing an increase of 3% over the existing rates of 60%" — taking DR to 63%. The Government put the cost of the DA and DR increase together at Rs 10,016.10 crore a year, benefiting about 48.54 lakh employees and 68.71 lakh pensioners. The DoPPW office memorandum that implements it for pensioners had not been published as of 30 October 2026; this page will cite it once it is out. The calculator pre-fills the official 60% → 63% change.

How DR and DR arrears are calculated

DR = (basic pension + additional pension for age) × DR %, rounded up to the next rupee
Monthly increase = DR at new rate − DR at old rate
Arrears = monthly increase × number of months from the date of effect up to the month before the new rate is first paid

  • Commutation does not reduce DR. DR is paid on the full basic pension; the commuted amount is deducted separately.
  • Fixed Medical Allowance (₹1,000 outside CGHS areas) does not attract DR.
  • Additional pension for pensioners aged 80 and above does attract DR.
  • Arrears are paid in the first month the new rate is credited, so that month's credit looks unusually high. Do not use it to work out your basic pension.

DR and the 8th Pay Commission

When the 7th CPC was implemented, the DR built up by then (125%) was absorbed into the new basic pension through the 2.57 fitment factor, and DR restarted from zero. If the 8th CPC follows the same pattern, every DR instalment granted until implementation (63% from July 2026, and the January 2027 and later instalments) raises the break-even fitment factor: at 60% DR a factor of 1.60 merely kept total pension unchanged; at today's 63%, 1.63. That is why the main 8th CPC calculator lets you change the DR box, and why every DR decision matters for the estimate. The Government told Parliament in December 2025 that there was no proposal to merge DA with basic pay.

Who should use this calculator and how

Any central government pensioner or family pensioner — civil, railway, defence or All India Services — who wants to check the DR in their credit or estimate the effect of a new instalment.

  1. Enter your basic pension (before commutation) and age.
  2. The official change is pre-filled: 60% old rate, 63% new rate from 1 July 2026 (Cabinet decision 28 October 2026). For a later instalment, enter it as the new rate once announced.
  3. Choose the month the new rate takes effect (July 2026 for this instalment) and the month you expect it to be credited (typically November 2026).
  4. Read the monthly increase and arrears. If you entered a commutation deduction, the net credit is shown too.

Common mistakes

  • Calculating DR on the reduced pension. DR is paid on the full basic pension before commutation.
  • Adding DR on FMA. Fixed Medical Allowance does not attract DR.
  • Believing a "DA hike" headline before the order. The Cabinet decision appears on pib.gov.in; DoPPW then issues the DR order for pensioners.

Worked example: a pensioner aged 85

Additional pension also attracts DR, so older pensioners gain a little more from each instalment. For a basic pension of ₹20,000 at age 85, additional pension is 30% (₹6,000). DR at the official 63% (from 1 July 2026) on ₹26,000 is ₹16,380, against ₹15,600 at 60% — ₹780 more a month, or ₹3,120 for four months of arrears (July–October) if the new rate is first paid with the November pension, as is typical. The same pensioner below 80 gains ₹600 a month.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current DR for central government pensioners?

63% of basic pension (including additional pension) from 1 July 2026, following the Cabinet decision of 28.10.2026 (PIB release ID 2329485). It was 60% from 1 January 2026 under DoPPW OM dated 24.04.2026.

Has the July 2026 DA/DR been announced?

Yes. The Union Cabinet approved it on 28 October 2026: DA and DR go from 60% to 63% with effect from 1 July 2026. The DoPPW order for pensioners was awaited as of 30 October 2026.

When will the July–October DR arrears be paid?

Typically with the first pension paid at the new rate, often November. It depends on when the DoPPW order is issued and your bank processes it. For ₹20,000 basic pension, 4 months of arrears is ₹2,400.

How are DR arrears calculated?

Arrears = (DR at the new rate − DR at the old rate) × the number of months from the date of effect until the month before the new rate is first paid. Each month's DR is rounded up to the next rupee.

Is DR paid on the commuted part of pension?

Yes. DR is calculated on the original basic pension before the commutation deduction.

Do family pensioners get the same DR?

Yes. The same DR percentage applies to family pension, including additional family pension for those aged 80 and above.

Is DR paid on Fixed Medical Allowance?

No. FMA of ₹1,000 a month is a flat allowance and does not attract DR.

How is DA/DR calculated from CPI-IW?

The 7th CPC formula uses the 12-month average of the All-India Consumer Price Index for Industrial Workers published by the Labour Bureau. The Government announces the rate; calculations by others are only estimates.

When is the July DA/DR usually announced?

There is no fixed date. The rate takes effect from 1 July, and the decision has often come in September–October, with arrears paid afterwards.

Do railway and defence pensioners get the same DR?

Yes. The DoPPW order applies the same rate to armed forces, railway and All India Services pensioners and family pensioners.

Is DR taxable?

DR forms part of pension income and is taxed with it under the normal rules; the bank deducts TDS where applicable.
